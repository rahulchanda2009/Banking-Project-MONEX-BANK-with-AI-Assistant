## Vehicle (Car) Loan – Eligibility, Steps and Contact
Eligibility rules:
- Age: 21 to 65 years.
- Minimum monthly income: Rs. 25,000.
- Down payment: at least 15% of the on-road price of the vehicle (Monex finances up to 85%).
- Loan amount (on-road price minus down payment) must not exceed 30 times the monthly income.
- Existing EMIs must be less than 40% of the monthly income.
- Indicative interest rate: 9.0% to 11.5% per year. Tenure: up to 7 years.

If eligible – next steps:
1. Choose the vehicle and get a proforma invoice from the dealer.
2. Keep documents ready: PAN, Aadhaar, last 3 months' salary slips or 2 years' ITR, last 6 months' bank statements, proforma invoice.
3. Contact the Vehicle Loans Desk to book a verification appointment.
4. Loan sanction usually within 48 hours of document verification.

If not eligible – suggestions:
- Increase the down payment, choose a lower-priced vehicle, or add a co-applicant with income.

## Home Loan – Eligibility, Steps and Contact
Eligibility rules:
- Age: 21 to 60 years.
- Minimum monthly salary: Rs. 30,000.
- Loan amount must not exceed 60 times the monthly salary.
- Existing EMIs must be less than 40% of the monthly salary.
- Indicative interest rate: 8.5% to 9.5% per year. Tenure: up to 30 years.

If eligible – next steps:
1. Shortlist the property and collect the sale agreement and property documents.
2. Keep documents ready: PAN, Aadhaar, last 6 months' salary slips, Form 16 for 2 years, last 6 months' bank statements, property papers.
3. Contact the Home Loans Specialist for a free eligibility review and property valuation.
4. In-principle approval in 3 working days; final sanction after legal and technical verification.

If not eligible – suggestions:
- Reduce the loan amount, close or reduce existing loans, or add a co-applicant (for example a spouse).

Contact: Priya Nair, Home Loans Specialist – Phone: +91 98765 43212

## Personal Loan – Eligibility, Steps and Contact
Eligibility rules:
- Age: 21 to 58 years.
- Minimum monthly income: Rs. 25,000 for salaried, Rs. 40,000 for self-employed.
- Loan amount must not exceed 20 times the monthly income, with a maximum of Rs. 25 lakh.
- Existing EMIs must be less than 50% of the monthly income.
- Indicative interest rate: 11% to 16% per year. Tenure: 1 to 5 years.

If eligible – next steps:
1. Keep documents ready: PAN, Aadhaar, last 3 months' salary slips (or 2 years' ITR if self-employed), last 3 months' bank statements.
2. Contact the Relationship Manager to complete the application.
3. Disbursal within 24 hours of approval for existing Monex customers.

If not eligible – suggestions:
- Request a smaller amount or a longer tenure, or reduce existing EMIs first.
